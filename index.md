# Summary
The DART (Demonstrator for Autonomous Rapid Transport) project aims to demonstrate the feasibility of using rocket vehicles for point-to-point payload deliver. The project stems from a desire from the United States Space Force (USSF) to deliver global payloads in less time than required by conventional means (i.e., cargo planes). Furthermore, the project derives importance and relevance from the aerospace industry's trend towards reusable rocketry.

# Problem Statement
Although conventional cargo planes can carry large payloads long distances, the time they require to do so often limits their usefulness. Accordingly, the USSF has identified a need to deliver global payloads in a much shorter time via reusable rockets. This team is challenged with launching and propulsively landing a reusable rocket capable of delivering a payload with a <u>horizontal displacement of at least 50 meters.</u>

# Project Objectives
- OBJ-01: The team shall design and build a reusable rocket capable of launching and propulsively landing.

- OBJ-02: The team shall design and implement a control system capable of autonomously operating and controlling the rocket during flight and landing.

- OBJ-03: The team shall design the rocket to integrate and fly a payload with a mass of at least 75 grams.

- OBJ-04: The team shall perform a complete test flight of the rocket (launch and landing) before the 2025 Senior Design Showcase.
